# DECISIONS

Architecture and art-direction decisions, newest first. Each entry: what was decided, why, and what it supersedes. The Notion "afar — MAS Design Brief" is the founding spec; entries here marked **override** take precedence over it.

## 2026-07-31

- **Plain-language rule for every public surface (Gabe — standing content rule).** Everything user-facing must be clear to a reader with no AI background and no music-production background: zero unexplained jargon, while keeping the serif art-label voice (elegant, never dumbed-down or corporate). Concretely: "multi-agent"/"agents" → "musicians made of software" / "acts" / their names ("AI" alone is fine); "intent" → "what they set out to make"; "rationale" → "in their own words"; technical titles ("The interaction record") survive only when immediately followed by a one-line plain explanation ("who pulled whom…"); condition codes (contact/isolation/parallel) stay as short chips but carry a plain tooltip; common record-world words (era, set, take, release, roster) stay, with surrounding copy carrying the meaning. The acts' own generated lines/lyrics/rationales are quoted material and are never rewritten. Applies to pages, metadata, component labels, fixtures prose, and the authored release prose seeded to Neon. Under this rule the tagline becomes: **"Three musicians made of software. They write and record music around the clock, hearing and reacting to each other — and every release shows who influenced whom."** *(Supersedes the tagline below and the note that Csikszentmihalyi vocabulary survives in the Producer bio — "the acts incubate; the Producer precipitates" is replaced by a plain equivalent.)*

- **Tagline says what it is; drop the aphorism and "negotiation" (Gabe, two passes).** First "negotiation" was replaced with Csikszentmihalyi's **incubation** (*Creativity: Flow and the Psychology of Discovery and Invention* — the book whose systems model already shapes the masthead: Muse≈domain, Critic+Listener≈field, acts≈person); then the aphorism form itself was dropped for a plain description: **"An autonomous multi-agent creative AI system. Three acts make music continuously; every release ships with the record of who influenced whom."** Csikszentmihalyi vocabulary survives in-world (Producer bio: "The acts incubate; the Producer precipitates" — `precipitate_theme` being the codebase's own verb) but the site leads with what the system is.

- **Artist's-judgment override of the persona gate (Gabe, after auditioning the 30 post-fix clips):** the acts sound sufficiently distinct by ear; proceed to the SOCIAL set. The MERT gate at 0.90 still fails (intra 0.9091 vs inter 0.8768 — Marlowe/Lane co-cluster; Patina half-separated), while intent space separates at purity 1.00. Consequence: release interaction records lead with **intent-space** influence features (fully supported); audio-space features are computed and logged but flagged as weak until audio separation improves. The gate remains the bar for any *research-grade* audio-space influence claims.

- **music_v2 reads CHUNK styles; the global style arrays were silently dropped.** Root cause of the flat MERT gate (intra 0.9397 vs inter 0.9388 — audio effectively identical across personas while intent space separated at purity 1.0): `kernel/afar/mapping.py` was ported from a STALE `afar_music` checkout. music_v2's CompositionPlan is chunks-only — `positive_global_styles`/`negative_global_styles` are the music_v1 MusicPrompt schema and are ignored, as are top-level `context_adherence` and `respect_sections_durations`. Every AFAR track so far got only its lyrics: no palette, no BPM, no persona sound. Fix (ported from afar_music commits 9a02e1b + 5bff478): styles + `context_adherence` live ON the chunk; ranked candidates compete for a **7-positive / 4-negative style budget** (BPM, era lead token, lead genre ×2, vocal-pad leads, and the solo combo are guaranteed slots); only leans ≥0.6 ban the opposing pole; influence token count uses `ceil` so a 10% genre still contributes; lyrics are multi-line (≤8 lines, ≤180 chars/line — the API limit is per LINE); the request body carries ONLY `model_id` + `composition_plan`. Pilot after the fix (6 clips, 2/persona): MERT intra 0.9038 vs inter 0.8749 — a real gap where there was none — and per-persona tempo/timbre structure in plain DSP (rust ~99–103 BPM, low centroid; silt 117.5; keep 112.3).
- **Correction to the earlier "no style budget exists" reading:** the spec's 7/4 token budget was real all along — the earlier conclusion that mapping.ts had no budget came from reading the same stale checkout. When porting from `afar_music`, `git -C ../ai-music/afar_music pull` first; the oracle files (`mapping.test.ts`, `music.test.ts`) are the parity bar, not memory of them.
- **Image model: OpenAI `gpt-image-1`** (portraits + cover, `web/scripts/generate_images.mjs`) — the client afar_music already proved out, and AFAR's media table mirrors afar_music's pattern. JPEG at quality medium / compression 85; all images 1024×1024 because every render surface (the square portrait plate, the album cover) is square. Bytes live in Neon `media` content-addressed by sha256, same as audio; agents rows carry `imageUrl`, releases `coverUrl`, both `/api/media/<hash>`. *(PR: act-images.)*
- **Portrait prompt strategy: one series, three mediums.** A shared series clause (paper-toned, archival, small-press-monograph register, no text) keeps the three acts reading as one roster; each act's *medium* carries its stance — silt: layered oils with earlier passes showing through (accumulation); rust: flaking-emulsion degraded photograph (erosion); keep: clean composed film photo in evening light (continuity). Content comes from fixture stance + the newest logged intent's seedPrompt/era/visualStyle/lyricalObsessions (runs/ first, Neon tracks-row mirror as fallback) plus the per-act accent tones. The 0001 cover is the release's standing-water image with one register per act.
- **Fixtures carry the media URLs too; a client `ArtImage` onError fallback covers fixture mode**, where `/api/media` 404s — the Radar plate (or nothing, for covers) takes over instead of a broken-image glyph. Zero-env pages stay whole. Fixture JSON is now machine-canonical (2-space `JSON.stringify`) since the image script rewrites it.
- **Naming register:** staff/press prose uses surnames (Marlowe / Patina / Lane); the acts use first names for each other in rationales. *(PR #7.)*
- **Per-act accent tones** scoped via `data-act` CSS custom-property override: silt keeps the house ochre; rust `#94512e`; keep `#5f7261`. The Radar silhouette in a framed "specimen plate" is each act's portrait until AI portraits land. *(PR #7.)*
- **Each personality is its own band.** Delta Marlowe, Roan Patina, and Evers Lane are three independent acts; **AFAR is the label/scene around them**, not a band name. The staff (Muse/Producer/Critic/Listener) are the label's masthead. The acts still perceive and influence each other — three acts on one label responding to each other's work. *(Override of the spec's "band" framing — Gabe.)*
- **Album covers are AI-image covers** (Tunz-style, prompt-driven from era + palette + visualStyle). *(Override of the spec §2 "the cover is the drawn interaction record / no image model in the loop" conceit — Gabe. The interaction record remains a first-class release artifact on the page; it's no longer the cover itself.)*
- **Stage names are display-only.** silt → Delta Marlowe, rust → Roan Patina, keep → Evers Lane. Entity IDs, URLs (`/agent/silt`), log keys, and DB keys never change — the display name is a presentation layer. Mineral slugs remain visible as sub-identities.
- **Lyrics are a dedicated Intent field.** `intent.lyrics` (model-written from `lyricalObsessions`, multi-line, ~1.5 words/sec per afar_music's PR #12 finding) is what gets sung; `line` is only the chat-bubble narration; `rationale` only the page text. First generation batch sang the narration line and came out effectively instrumental — that's why.

## 2026-07-30

- **Monorepo.** Kernel (Python) and web (Next.js) both live in `gabelev/AFAR` (`kernel/` + `web/`). *(Supersedes the spec §7 note that the web layer stays in its own repo.)*
- **License: AGPL-3.0-or-later** (was MIT at repo creation) — matches the `ensemble` framework's deliberate network-clause intent for hosted autonomous services.
- **Naming resolution** (spec §10 open decision, closed): `ensemble` = framework; **AFAR** = this installation, public face afar.band; `afar_music` = the separate product, afar.music; "The Ensemble Effect" = the offline research project, not a repo.
- **Renderer: ElevenLabs `music_v2` first, behind a swappable `Renderer` protocol.** `continue_from` is in the signature but raises on ElevenLabs (never use `conditioning_ref` for continuity — verified to produce near-covers). `MusicPromptError` is never auto-retried; only transient 429 retries once. 2-slot concurrency semaphore (process-local; needs a cluster lease before multi-process Step C).
- **Python/TS mapping parity is oracle-enforced.** `kernel/afar/mapping.py` must pass the oracles ported from `afar_music/lib/generation/mapping.test.ts`. JS `Math.round` ≠ Python `round` (banker's): all rounding goes through `_js_round(x) = floor(x + 0.5)`.
- **Append-only JSONL is authoritative; Neon is a derived mirror.** Every row carries condition, code_sha, seed, renderer_version, prompt_sha. Perceptions log what the agent *saw*. Artifacts are content-addressed and immutable. Embeddings are derived and carry model_id. *(The spec's earlier byte-identical-replay gate is cut — the experiment runs separately — but these provenance invariants survive it.)*
- **The site renders with zero env.** `web/lib/data.ts` falls back to bundled fixtures when `DATABASE_URL` is unset or any query fails. A broken DB degrades the site to fixtures; it never breaks a page.
- **Audio bytes live in Neon `media` (BYTEA), streamed via `/api/media/[id]`.** Not Vercel Blob (inherited decision from afar_music, where the linked Blob store was private). The `blob_url` pointer indirection keeps the byte store swappable.
- **Vercel:** Root Directory = `web`; framework pinned to `nextjs` in `web/vercel.json` (the dashboard preset was "Other", which made Vercel expect a static `public/` dir — first two deploys failed on these two settings).
- **MERT persona-distinctness gate runs at threshold 0.90, purity w.r.t. act identity.** The spec said 0.85, but mold's FMA bench shows 0.85 only separated Folk; clean separation needed ≥0.90. Do not weaken the threshold to pass — a failure is a persona-prompt problem.
- **Intent-space features are versioned.** `intent_vector` (18-dim: era/10 + 7 sonic + 2 vocal + 8 hashed-genre buckets) is pinned as `INTENT_VECTOR_VERSION = "1"`; embeddings rows carry the version so features stay comparable across runs.
- **Personas are defined by aesthetic commitment only** (accumulation / erosion / continuity), never by their relationship to influence — influence behaviour must emerge. *(Spec §2 trap, adopted as law.)*
- **The boundary rule is a single chokepoint.** `build_context()` is the only place condition branches; staff act between sets on the frame; within a set players perceive only players. The world enters through the brief, never the ear.
